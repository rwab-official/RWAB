Building a Verifiable Digital Asset Infrastructure

Introduction

RWAB is a blockchain-based project designed to establish digital infrastructure, organize documentation, preserve records, and support the development of a digital asset. The project uses Polygon PoS, a network compatible with the Ethereum ecosystem that enables transactions and smart-contract information to be recorded. RWAB seeks to connect a digital asset with its supporting documentation and an on-chain information registry.

Supporting evidence and documentation file

RWAB maintains a supporting documentation file prepared to preserve, review, and develop information related to the project. It includes documentation attachments, an index of supporting records, project-status information, and technical history. This structure is intended to provide an orderly path for reviewing documents and adding valid evidence at later stages.

The project’s approach to supporting evidence:

  • Organized document retention
  • Recording digital fingerprints of documents
  • Enabling comparison of document versions
  • Developing an information file for assets and related documentation
  • Enabling independent review of documents and valuation

The value and amount of verifiable financial backing must be determined from ownership documents, asset records, independent confirmation, and a credible valuation. Document registration alone is not equivalent to bank confirmation or proof of collateral value.

Price display and dollar value

RWAB’s commercial objective is to make token information and price viewable in wallets compatible with the network. Dollar-price display in a wallet depends on wallet support and the availability of a price-information source, market, or liquidity pool.

At present, reliable dollar-price registration in a wallet and confirmation that RWAB is displayed there are not documented in this file. A tradable price must come from a real market and completed trades—not merely from the number of tokens or a stated nominal value.

Exchange and liquidity

By design, RWAB is within the Polygon ecosystem, and developing the ability to exchange it through decentralized exchanges and liquidity pools may form part of the project’s development plan. A real swap requires an active pool, a trading pair, sufficient liquidity, and a usable trading route. Liquidity, market depth, and the ability to exit a trade directly affect the executable price and the amount that can be bought or sold. Until an active pool is confirmed and a trade is tested, RWAB swaps for other cryptoassets are not represented as an active feature.

RWAB development roadmap

  1. Complete and maintain the supporting documentation file.
  2. Complete independent review of asset and ownership records.
  3. Prepare a credible, verifiable valuation report.
  4. Review and complete the token’s technical information.
  5. Provide price-display infrastructure for compatible wallets.
  6. Create and test a liquidity pool if the necessary resources are available.
  7. Develop exchange routes and market access.
